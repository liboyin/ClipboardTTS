# S12 — Derive the player's unscheduled bytes from the buffered PCM

**Origin:** S8's implementation agent noticed that `AudioPlayerManager.pcmData.unprocessed` always equaled the bytes of `pcmData.accumulated` after its last complete frame (see [S8](S8.md)). On 2026-11-15 the user accepted S8's carried-byte test gap without change and asked for this simplification to be recorded as a task and executed. It was recorded in `71abbf8`.

## Accepted boundary

The boundary was kept in the task's working review record.

- **Intent:** the player keeps one PCM buffer. `scheduleAudio` schedules the frames completed since the previous buffered frame count instead of maintaining a second buffer.
- **Compatibility surface:** menu, Settings, Services, clipboard, persistence, and provider-request contracts are untouched. Audio-session contracts are preserved: exact frame accounting, the trailing-byte rule, the 0.1-second prebuffer, seek/replay over the retained buffer, underrun release, generation checks, and buffer-queue confinement.
- **Non-goals:** no change to `seek` (NB55), to published state, or to the buffer's lifetime; no new state.
- **Validation and done:** existing suites pass unchanged. Mutation evidence covers dropped, duplicated, and rescheduled frames, a range that ends short, and the clear in `stop()`. A survivor in the scheduling range would need a focused test. The gates pass, and the change is reviewed and archived.

**Revalidation at `71abbf8`:** `scheduleAudio` was the only writer of both buffers and `stop()` the only place that cleared them. Every `scheduleAudio` block that passes its guards removes all complete frames from `unprocessed`, whether it schedules them or fails to allocate their buffer, and sets `bufferedPCMFrameCount` to `accumulated.count / 2`. A block that fails a guard appends nothing. `seek` reads only `accumulated` and the frame count. So at the start of every block, `unprocessed` was `accumulated` from the counted frames onwards, and the frames a block completes are exactly those between the count before and after its append.

## Implementation

**S12 — Done.** The main agent made the change directly; at about ten lines, an implementation agent's handoff cost was not justified.

- `pcmData` is now one `Data`, with a doc comment: it keeps the whole stream for seek and replay, and only its complete frames are ever scheduled.
- `scheduleAudio` records `bufferedPCMFrameCount` before appending, then schedules `pcmData.subdata(in: previous·2 ..< new·2)`. A comment states why that range is exactly the newly completed frames, including one finished by a carried byte.
- `stop()` clears the one buffer, and two comments in `seek` name `pcmData`. TODO's NB55 reference was updated to match.

No test changed.
