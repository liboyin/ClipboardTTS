# S11 — Shorten DESIGN and README to the content each owns

**Origin:** the second full-project review of 2026-11-03 (TODO Evidence provenance) found DESIGN.md at about 11,700 words, in paragraphs of up to about 6,200 characters, much of it restating rationale already in source doc comments and `check-coverage.sh`'s header, and README's Mutation Testing section holding one 653-word paragraph (then `README.md:71`) that specified the runner's locking, signal, and deadline semantics. It was recorded as a proposal, not approved. On 2026-11-15, after the S5, S6, and S9 group landed, the user asked for S11 to be executed.

## Accepted boundary

- **Intent:** DESIGN keeps current architecture, design assumptions, invariants, concurrency rules, coverage-gate rationale in summary, test-harness design, and decision pointers, naming the owning code for detail; README keeps commands, inputs, outputs, and exit codes, naming the runner's docstring and functions for the rest. Long paragraphs are split where that helps reading.
- **Removal rule:** a fact may be removed or condensed only when (a) its owner (a source or test-helper doc comment, `check-coverage.sh`, `run-mutants.py`, `verify-mutation-runner.py`, `.swiftlint.yml`, another document, or a completed_work record for history) already states it and the text names that owner, or (b) it is history that does not describe current behavior and its decision stays recorded in TODO or completed_work with its pointer kept. Content with no other owner stays, though it may be reworded more tightly. Nothing moves into source or scripts: only DESIGN.md and README.md change.
- **Must survive:** every accepted safeguard, test-ownership and isolation rule, concurrency and lock-order rule, coverage-exclusion rationale and README's smoke check for each, every applicable decision reference, every command, flag, exit code, and version or environment claim, and every heading other documents link to. No fact changes and no new claim, except that DESIGN describes [S5](S5.md)'s per-render catalog lookup.
- **Non-goals:** executable files and configuration, TODO's other entries, and other completed_work records.
- **Validation and done:** both files materially shorter; a removal inventory checked row by row against the cited owners by an agent that did not write it; links, anchors, and one source line per paragraph checked. Documentation-only, so AGENTS exempts it from the application gates and formal adversarial review.

**Revalidation at `05d18be`:** DESIGN.md was 11,915 words with a longest paragraph of 6,213 characters (950 words); README.md was 3,077 words, with the Mutation Testing paragraph at 653 words and a longest paragraph of 4,395 characters.
