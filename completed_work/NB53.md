# NB53 — The loopback WebSocket server's shutdown accounts for every connection and receive

**Origin:** the 2026-11-03 full-project review of `0ccae84`. The test-only `LoopbackWebSocketServer` in `Tests/RealtimeSpeechConnectionTests.swift` cancelled its listener and connections on `requestStop()`, but `awaitStopped` observed only the listener's `.cancelled` followed by a queue drain. Nothing acknowledged a connection's cancellation or accounted for a pending receive, and a late successful receive could still record a frame and arm another. Executed as the second of a two-task group with [NB52](NB52.md), one task per commit.

**Accepted boundary:** intent: `awaitStopped` reports success only when the listener, every accepted connection, and every armed receive have quiesced; once stopping begins, no connection is armed and no receive is recorded or re-armed. Compatibility surface: test-only; nothing under `Sources/` changes, and `testTheWebSocketAdapterSendsEachClientEventAsATextFrame` keeps proving the production `URLSessionWebSocketTask` conformance sends a text frame, with its teardown deadline, recovery bound, and run-ending behavior unchanged. Every connection stays on the `127.0.0.1` literal. Non-goals: NB52, NB58, NB61, the S proposals, and the server's handling of a peer's end of stream before stopping. Validation: verify Network.framework's cancellation behavior first; then prove that a held connection cancellation or receive completion keeps shutdown incomplete until it is released, and cover a late accepted connection, timeout recovery, repeated shutdown, a fully stopped server, and a server never started; mutants for listener-only completion, missing receive accounting, re-arming after stopping, arming a late connection, and over-restriction. Done when those pass, gates and review pass, and the TODO entry is archived.

**Revalidation at `0d67324`:** as described above. The connections had no state handler, `newConnectionHandler` armed every connection whenever it arrived, and `receive(on:)` re-armed after every message without checking for shutdown.

**Network.framework behavior verified:** the implementation agent ran a scratch loopback probe on macOS 26.7.1 (25G241) on 2026-11-03; it is not checked in. Cancelling a ready server-side connection completes its pending `receiveMessage` *before* `.cancelled`, with no content, `isComplete` true and **no error**, so the old server would have recorded it as a frame and re-armed. On a connection not yet ready, that completion carries `ECANCELED`, and a receive armed after cancellation, even after `.cancelled`, still completes later with `ECANCELED`. A started connection reports `.cancelled`; one cancelled before it was started reports no state at all. `newConnectionHandler` ran for a queued connection after `listener.cancel()` and before the listener's `.cancelled`, and a connection attempted after `.cancelled` was refused.

**NB53 — Fixed.** The server moves to `Tests/LoopbackWebSocketServer.swift` and is made internal, beside a pure `LoopbackServerLedger`. The ledger tracks whether the listener is live, the accepted connections not yet `.cancelled`, the outstanding receives, a stopping flag, and a count of receives armed. Every callback updates it on the server's serial queue. `requestStop()` marks the ledger stopping, then cancels every connection and the listener. A connection accepted after stopping begins is started, so that it reports `.cancelled`, and cancelled without being armed. A receive completion settles its outstanding receive, and only a successful one before stopping is recorded and re-armed. `awaitStopped` waits, on a semaphore signalled after each update, until the ledger is quiescent and no callback is held, reading both on the queue. A test-only `Interceptor` can hold chosen callbacks until `releaseHeldCallbacks()`. The teardown helper became the file-level `finishStoppingOrEndRun`, unchanged in behavior. `Tests/LoopbackWebSocketServerTests.swift` holds five pure `LoopbackServerLedgerTests` and six `LoopbackWebSocketServerTests` that drive a real server with a Network.framework client of their own on the loopback literal: a server never started, a fully stopped server that stays stopped through a repeated stop, a held connection cancellation, a held receive completion, a held new connection, and a connection accepted after stop. Each held case shows shutdown incomplete, then complete after release, which is the timeout-recovery path. DESIGN's test-harness paragraph names the server's guarantee.

**Assumption:** the guarantee relies on the listener delivering nothing after its own `.cancelled`. The probe is consistent with that, but Network.framework does not document it; the server's documentation says so.

**Validation:** macOS 26.7.1 (25G241) arm64, Xcode 27.2 (27B52), SwiftLint 0.65.1, XcodeGen 2.46.0. Every gate below ran `xcodegen generate` after deleting the project's derived data, and built the Debug app with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO SWIFT_STRICT_CONCURRENCY=complete` into fresh derived data.

The fix is that held callbacks count as outstanding work, and the reviewer's probe became `testAHeldNewConnectionKeepsShutdownIncompleteAfterTheListenerStops`.

**Limitations:**

- The listener assumption above is undocumented by Apple.
- The "incomplete while held" checks call `awaitStopped(timeout: 0.1)`. A correct server returns false however long that timeout is, because the hold is deterministic; only a broken server's failure depends on it.
- `late-connection-armed-in-server` is killed only because the stray receive completes before `.cancelled`, the order the probe observed.
- Before stopping, a completion with no error and no content, a peer's end of stream, is still recorded as `.other` and re-armed, as it was before. That was outside the boundary.
