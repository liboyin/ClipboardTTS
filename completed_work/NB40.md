**Limitations.** The three log reads use the locale's encoding rather than UTF-8. Under a UTF-8, `C`, or unset locale both interpreters read them as UTF-8 (probed; Python's UTF-8 mode or C-locale coercion applies), but under a Latin-1 locale such as `en_US.ISO8859-1` a byte never fails to decode, a non-ASCII test name would be misreported in `KILLED` details, and the non-ASCII assertions of `test_output_that_is_not_utf8_is_still_judged` would fail; the verdicts themselves would not change. That observation is recorded separately as NB43 and was out of this boundary. A byte inside a terminal marker line would make it no marker under replacement, which is not a shape `xcodebuild` produces and no case holds. The stand-in generator's message is produced by `/bin/sh`'s `printf` octal escapes.
