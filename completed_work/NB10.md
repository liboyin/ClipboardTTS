# NB10 — Fractional Custom sample rates survive Settings

Moved from [TODO.md](../TODO.md) on 2026-09-24 without rewording; the text below is the disposition as TODO recorded it, and its evidence is tied to the revisions it names.

**NB10 — Fixed.** Settings now hydrates Custom sample-rate text from `Double`'s round-trip representation, removing only a terminal whole-number `.0`. A valid fractional rate therefore survives mount and reopen unchanged rather than being saved back through an integer draft or rebuilding the graph at a different rate. The hosted owned-defaults regression reads the field without editing it, asserts its 24000.4 draft, persistence, and graph immediately after mount and reopen, and proves an unrelated ordinary settings sync preserves the same rate. Reverting the integer formatter, rounding to three significant digits, restricting rates to integers, and rounding the synchronized target each fail it in isolated worktrees. README owns the current behavior.
