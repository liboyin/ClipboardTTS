# NB51 — `gpt-realtime-2.1-mini` intermittently goes silent mid-response while reporting success

**Priority:** Non-blocking. Needs the user's decision on the evidence.

**Validated — live trials, 2026-11-02, outside the repository.** Python `websockets` clients reproduced the app's session settings: read-verbatim instructions, `alloy`, 24-kHz PCM, no turn detection. In 11 of 34 mini responses (500 to 12,000 characters, sequential and concurrent), the voice read normally and then produced at least 15 s of near-silence (5-s RMS below 400, against about 3,500 for speech), usually for the rest of the response; a 12th ended with about 6 s of trailing silence that may be ordinary padding, while `response.done` reported `completed` and the text transcript claimed every word was read. Whisper transcription of one affected 12,000-character response recovered about 141 of 2,080 words. `gpt-realtime-2.1` had 0 of 6 affected responses (1,000 and 4,000 characters). **Limitations:** those inputs were synthetic, repetitive sentences ("Note N: …"); sample sizes are small; the app cannot detect the defect from protocol events. **Natural-prose follow-up, 2026-11-02:** five distinct README passages of 1,709–2,053 characters, read once by each model with the app's session settings, produced no silent 15-s stretch in any of the 10 responses (0 of 5 for mini, against 3 of 4 synthetic mini runs at about 2,000 characters), and Whisper recovered 93–97% word agreement for every response, the shortfall being spoken identifiers and symbols. The defect may therefore be triggered by repetitive input rather than ordinary prose; five runs per model cannot rule it out. **Direction:** decide whether this evidence is enough to close NB51, or gather more natural-prose runs; only if the defect recurs on ordinary text, choose between the default model (D30), client-side silence detection with re-request, or shorter per-response chunks. **Acceptance:** DESIGN's limitation states current evidence, and any mitigation has a test that fails when a silent response is reported as success.
